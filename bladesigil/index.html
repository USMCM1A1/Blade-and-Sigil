Battle

Walking into a monster (or getting caught by one) moves the fight to a tactical battlefield: every nearby monster joins, and each hero and monster stands on their own square. Initiative (d20 + DEX) sets the turn order. On a hero's turn: arrow keys move (4 squares), and walking into a monster attacks it — that ends the turn. C opens a caster's spell menu and F draws the archer's bow — aim with the arrows, Enter to unleash. Beware: a fireball burns friend and foe alike! Space ends the turn; Esc flees. Front-row heroes start closest to the enemy. Every swing appears in the message log.
