Battle

Walking into a monster (or getting caught by one) moves the fight to a tactical battlefield: every nearby monster joins, and each hero and monster stands on their own square. Initiative (d20 + DEX) sets the turn order. On a hero's turn: arrow keys move (4 squares), and walking into a monster attacks it — that ends the turn. C opens a caster's spell menu, F draws the archer's bow — aim with the arrows, Enter to unleash — and I drinks a potion from the party pouch (that's the hero's action). Beware: a fireball burns friend and foe alike! Space ends the turn; Esc flees — every enemy beside a hero gets a free strike at their backs, then the foes regroup for a few turns while you run: use that head start! Wounded monsters that catch you again start the rematch right on top of you. Front-row heroes start closest to the enemy. Every swing appears in the message log.
